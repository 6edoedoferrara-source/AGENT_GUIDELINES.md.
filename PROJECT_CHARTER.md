DOS – PROJECT CHARTER

Project: Digital Operations Studio
Version: 1.0
Status: Initial Draft

1. Purpose

DOS is a long-term project to develop a scalable digital operations platform powered by controlled AI capabilities.

The platform will support multiple business sectors through one shared engine and configurable product packages.

The purpose is to create reusable digital services, reduce repetitive operational work and progressively develop a sustainable digital business.

2. Strategic objectives

- Build one reusable platform rather than separate applications.
- Develop and validate commercially useful digital products.
- Minimize initial investment and operating costs.
- Automate repeatable processes without sacrificing quality.
- Preserve data integrity, security and customer trust.
- Expand into additional sectors using the same central architecture.

3. Initial sectors

The initial sectors are:

1. E-commerce and online sellers.
2. Hospitality and accommodation.
3. Local businesses.
4. Independent professionals.

4. First commercial products

The initial product experiments are:

- Guest Communication Kit.
- Seller Operations Assistant.
- Local Business Communication Pack.
- Professional Client Onboarding Pack.

These are proposed products. Their commercial viability must be tested through actual customer demand and feedback.

5. Development philosophy

The project will follow incremental development.

The order of priorities is:

1. Validate the architecture.
2. Build the shared foundations.
3. Test the first product.
4. Validate commercial demand.
5. Improve automation and efficiency.
6. Expand only when justified.

Avoid unnecessary spending and technical complexity.

6. Roles and responsibilities

Project Owner

Owns the project and approves strategic decisions, spending, external access and production deployment.

ChatGPT – Architecture Lead

Develops specifications, coordinates technical reasoning and reviews engineering outputs.

Codex – Engineering Agent

Analyzes the repository, performs approved engineering tasks, runs tests and produces technical reports.

7. Financial principles

The initial development phase should use free or low-cost tools wherever practical.

No paid service, subscription, hosting commitment or advertising expense may be introduced without explicit owner approval.

Every proposed investment must include:

- Cost
- Expected benefit
- Risks
- Free or cheaper alternatives
- Estimated recovery period

8. Decision authority

Technical recommendations may be developed collaboratively.

Final authority over strategic direction, budget, external permissions and deployment remains with the Project Owner.

Codex may recommend changes but cannot independently authorize them.

9. Initial milestone

The first milestone is successful completion and review of DOS-001: Independent Architecture Review.

No substantial implementation is authorized until the architecture review has been evaluated and the next development phase explicitly approved.

10. Success criteria

DOS should progressively demonstrate:

- Reusable architecture.
- Reliable processing.
- Traceable data.
- Controlled AI integration.
- Consistent output quality.
- Lower production effort through reuse.
- Evidence of commercial demand.
- Sustainable operating costs.

Project principle: Build carefully, validate early, spend only when justified and preserve one coherent platform as the business expands.
